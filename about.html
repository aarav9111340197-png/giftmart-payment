Our Mission

Making digital gifting simple, secure, and instant for everyone.

Who We Are

Founded in 2024, GiftMart started with a simple idea: digital gift cards should be as easy to send as a text message. We've built a platform that connects users with the world's most popular brands, ensuring that you can find the perfect gift in seconds.

Why We Exist

Traditional gift cards are often bulky, easy to lose, and restricted to physical locations. Digital gift cards solve these problems, but finding a trusted source can be difficult. GiftMart bridges that gap by providing a verified marketplace with industry-leading security and instant delivery.

Our Values

  • Security: Your data is protected by the latest encryption.
  • Speed: Instant delivery is our standard, not an option.
