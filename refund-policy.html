Refund & Cancellation

Transparent policies for your peace of mind.

1. Digital Goods Policy

At GiftMart, we provide digital gift cards. Due to the nature of digital goods, **all sales are final once the code has been delivered** to your email address. Once a digital code is exposed or delivered, it cannot be returned or refunded.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are only eligible under the following circumstances:

  • The payment was successful but the gift card code was never delivered (technical failure).
  • The delivered code is verified as invalid or already redeemed prior to your purchase.
  • The transaction was a verified duplicate (charged twice for the same order).

3. Failed Payments & Duplicate Transactions

If your payment fails but the amount is deducted from your bank, the refund is usually processed automatically by your bank within **24-48 hours**.

In case of duplicate transactions, please contact our support team immediately with your Transaction IDs (UTR). Verified duplicate charges will be refunded to the original payment source.

4. Processing Time

Once a refund is approved by our team, it will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank/UPI) within **5–7 business days**. Please note that bank holidays may affect this timeline.

5. Fraud Prevention

To prevent fraudulent activity, we monitor all transactions. Any attempt to claim a refund for a successfully redeemed code or through false chargebacks will result in a permanent ban from our platform and possible legal action.

Contact Support

If you need assistance with a refund, please reach out to us at support@giftmart.example.com with your order details. Our team is available 24/7.
